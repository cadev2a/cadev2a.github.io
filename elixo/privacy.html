Privacy Policy

Elixo game · Effective November 16, 2026

In short
  • No account, no sign-up. cadev2a collects no personal data.
  • Your progress and settings stay on your phone.
  • The game is free thanks to Google AdMob ads: a short video, only if you choose it, to get an extra tube, and now and then an ad between two levels.
  • Google may process some data to show these ads. In Europe, you are asked for your consent and can change it at any time.

1. Who we are

Elixo (“the game”) is published by cadev2a. For any question about this policy or your data, write to contact.cadev2a@gmail.com.

2. What stays on your phone

The game only keeps, on your device:

  • your progress (levels cleared, stars, achievements, daily challenges, game in progress);
  • your settings (sound, vibration, language, tube style, colour-reading aids).

This information is not sent anywhere and is deleted if you delete the game. The “progress code”, used to move your game to another phone, is made on your device: you copy it and type it in yourself, and it never goes through any cadev2a server.

3. What cadev2a does not do

  • no account, no sign-up, no email address requested;
  • no phone permission requested (no microphone, photos, location or contacts);
  • no analytics or tracking tool of its own;
  • no selling or renting of data.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Ads fund the game's development, its fixes and its future levels. We keep them low-key:

  • rewarded video: only if you tap “+ Tube”, to get an extra empty tube;
  • ad between two levels: never before level 15, never in Zen mode, and at most about once every 5 minutes of play.

Ads are provided by Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited for Europe), acting for its own purposes. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may process:

Your consent. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, a short Elixo page followed by a Google message asks for your consent on first launch. 5. Children

Elixo is a puzzle game for everyone and is not specifically aimed at children. Ads are limited to content suitable for a broad audience. cadev2a does not knowingly collect any data about children.

6. Your rights

As cadev2a holds no data about you, there is nothing to access, correct or delete on its side. For data processed by Google for advertising, you can exercise your rights with Google and manage your choices as described in section 4. You can also write to us at contact.cadev2a@gmail.com, and, if you live in France, lodge a complaint with the CNIL.

7. Changes

This policy may change, for example if a feature is added. The effective date at the top of the page is then updated. For a significant change, the game will let you know.
