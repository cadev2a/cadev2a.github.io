Support

A bug, a level that seems impossible, an idea? Write to contact.cadev2a@gmail.com with the level number, your phone model and its version (iOS or Android).

Frequently asked questions

I'm stuck on a level.

Tap “Hint” to see a good move, “Undo” to go back, or “+ Tube” to get an extra empty tube (once per attempt). Every level has a solution.

Why are there ads?

They fund the game's development, its fixes and its future levels. The “+ Tube” video only plays if you choose it; other ads never come before level 15, never in Zen mode, and at most about once every 5 minutes of play. When offline, the extra tube is free.

How do I change my ad choices?

In the game: Settings → Ad choices (in Europe).

How do I keep my progress on a new phone?

In the game, open “Transfer my progress” on the old phone, copy the code, then paste it in the same place on the new one.
