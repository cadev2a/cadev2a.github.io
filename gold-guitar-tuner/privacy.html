Privacy Policy

Gold Guitar Tuner app · Effective November 11, 2026

In short
  • Microphone sound is analyzed live on your phone. It is never recorded or sent.
  • No account, no sign-up. cadev2a collects no personal data.
  • Your settings stay on your phone.
  • A Google AdMob banner is shown at the bottom of the screen. Google may process some data to show it; in Europe you are asked for consent and can change your choice at any time.

1. Who we are

Gold Guitar Tuner (“the app”) is published by cadev2a. For any question about this policy or your data, write to contact.cadev2a@gmail.com.

2. The microphone

The app needs the microphone to hear your guitar. Sound is analyzed in real time, directly on your phone, to work out the note. It is then immediately discarded:

  • it is never recorded, kept or sent to anyone, including cadev2a;
  • the microphone is only used while the tuner is on screen and is released as soon as the app goes to the background.

You can revoke microphone access at any time in your phone settings. The tuner then cannot work.

3. What stays on your phone

The app only keeps your settings: confirmation sound, cents display, left-handed mode and reference A frequency. They stay on your device, are sent nowhere and are erased if you delete the app.

4. What cadev2a does not do

  • no account, no sign-up, no email address requested;
  • no analytics or tracking tool of its own;
  • no selling or renting of data.

6. Children

The app is meant for all musicians and is not specifically aimed at children. cadev2a does not knowingly collect any data about children.

7. Your rights

Since cadev2a holds no data about you, there is nothing to access, correct or delete on its side. For data processed by Google for advertising, you can exercise your rights with Google and manage your choices as described in section 5. You can also write to contact.cadev2a@gmail.com, and lodge a complaint with your data protection authority.

8. Changes

This policy may change, for example if a feature is added. The effective date at the top of this page is then updated. For a significant change, the app will let you know.
