Frequently asked questions

How do I tune my guitar?

Open the app and play an open string. In automatic mode, the tuner recognizes the string. The needle shows whether the note is too low (left) or too high (right). Turn the tuning peg until the needle is centered and turns green: the string is in tune when the ring fills up.

Automatic or manual mode?

In automatic mode, the app detects which string you play. In manual mode, you pick the string by tapping its peg on the headstock — handy for a badly detuned string. The button at the top right switches between modes.

The tuner hears nothing

  • Check that microphone access is allowed: phone Settings → Gold Guitar Tuner → Microphone.
  • Bring the phone closer to the guitar and pluck the string firmly.
  • If a call or another app is using the microphone, the tuner resumes on its own once it is free.

The app asks me to play the string again

The note is too far from every string to be assigned without risk of error. Switch to manual mode, pick the string, and tune it up slowly: the needle will guide you.

My other tuner does not show exactly the same value

A plucked string starts slightly sharp, then drops a few cents as it fades, especially the low strings. Gold Guitar Tuner follows the note to the end, while some tuners keep the value from the start. On a steady reference tone, the values are identical.

Can I tune to A = 442 Hz?

Yes: Settings → Reference A, from 430 to 450 Hz.

I'm left-handed

Settings → Left-handed mode: the headstock is mirrored.

The confirmation sound does not play

Turn it on in the app settings. It stays silent when the phone is on silent mode.

How do I change my advertising choice?

Settings → Ad privacy. For details, see the privacy policy.
