Privacy Policy

Sketchpine app · Effective November 13, 2026

In short
  • Your drawings stay on your device. They are never sent to cadev2a or anyone else.
  • No account, no sign-up, no advertising, no analytics. cadev2a collects no data at all.
  • When you import a picture, the app only receives the photo you choose.

1. Who we are

Sketchpine (“the app”) is a drawing app published by cadev2a. For any question about this policy, write to contact.cadev2a@gmail.com.

2. Your drawings

Drawings, their layers, their previous versions and the trash are saved in the app's private storage on your device. They are not uploaded anywhere. They may be included in your device backups (iCloud or computer), which you control in your device settings. Deleting the app deletes them.

3. Importing a picture

To import a picture, the app uses Apple's photo picker. The app does not ask for access to your photo library: it only receives the single photo you select, which becomes a layer of your drawing on your device.

4. Exporting

When you export or share a drawing, the image goes where you choose (Files, Photos, another app). The app sends nothing on its own.

5. What stays on your device

Your settings, favorite tools, saved colors and tool settings stay on your device and are erased if you delete the app.

6. What cadev2a does not do

  • no account, no sign-up, no email address requested;
  • no advertising, no analytics or tracking tool;
  • no collection, selling or sharing of any data.

7. Test versions (TestFlight)

If you test a pre-release version through Apple's TestFlight, Apple may share with cadev2a the information TestFlight provides to developers (for example your feedback, crash reports and the email you used for the invitation), according to Apple's privacy policy. cadev2a uses it only to improve the app.

8. Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no data, including about children.

9. Changes

This policy may change if a feature is added. The effective date at the top of this page is then updated.
