Support

Need help with Sketchpine, found a bug, or have an idea? Write to contact.cadev2a@gmail.com. Please mention your device model and iOS version.

Frequent questions

Where are my drawings saved?

On your device, automatically, a fraction of a second after each stroke. Nothing is sent online.

I made a mistake and it was already saved.

Open the … menu, then Previous versions: a version is kept every few minutes of drawing and always before a big erase or a deleted layer.

I deleted a drawing.

It is in the trash: My drawings → Trash, then touch and hold it and choose Restore.

How do I go back to 100% zoom and a straight canvas?

Open the … menu, then Zoom and rotation, and choose Back to 100% and 0°.
