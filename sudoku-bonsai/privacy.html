Privacy policy

Sudoku Bonsaï game for iPhone and iPad · Effective November 18, 2026

In short
  • No account, no sign-up. cadev2a collects no personal data.
  • Your grids, statistics and settings stay on your iPhone or iPad.
  • The game is free thanks to Google AdMob advertising: a small banner at the bottom of the screen, and a short video only if you choose to watch one (extra hint, second chance).
  • Google may process some data to show these ads. In Europe, you are asked for your consent and can change it at any time.

1. Who we are

Sudoku Bonsaï (“the game”), for iPhone and iPad, is published by cadev2a. For any question about this policy or your data, write to contact.cadev2a@gmail.com.

2. What stays on your iPhone or iPad

The game only keeps, on your device:

  • the grid in progress and the daily challenge grid;
  • your completed challenges, statistics per level, best scores, day streak and history of finished grids;
  • your settings (game assists, display, haptics, keep screen on).

This information is not sent anywhere and is erased if you delete the game. Grids are made inside the game itself: it needs no cadev2a server.

3. What cadev2a does not do

  • no account, no sign-up, no email address requested;
  • no device permission requested (no microphone, photos, location or contacts);
  • no analytics or tracking tool of its own;
  • no selling or renting of data.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Advertising funds the game's development, fixes and new features. We keep it discreet:

  • banner: a small banner at the bottom of the screen;
  • rewarded video: only if you choose it, to get a hint after the free one each grid, or a second chance after three mistakes. If no video is available, the help is free;
  • no full-screen ad ever interrupts a grid.

Ads are provided by Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited in Europe), acting for its own purposes. To show ads, measure them and fight fraud, Google may process:

5. Children

Sudoku Bonsaï is a puzzle game for everyone and is not specifically aimed at children. Ads are limited to content suitable for a general audience. cadev2a does not knowingly collect any data about children.

6. Your rights

As cadev2a holds no data about you, there is nothing to access, correct or delete on its side. For data processed by Google for advertising, you can exercise your rights with Google and manage your choices as described in section 4. You can also write to us at contact.cadev2a@gmail.com, and, if you live in France, lodge a complaint with the CNIL.

7. Changes

This policy may change, for example if a feature is added. The effective date at the top of the page is then updated. For an important change, the game will let you know.
