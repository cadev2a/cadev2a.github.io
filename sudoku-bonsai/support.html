Frequently asked questions

I'm stuck on a grid.

Tap “Hint”: the game first shows you where to look, then the number if you want. One hint is free each grid; further hints need a short video. “Auto notes” fills in every possibility in empty cells. Every grid has a single solution that logic can find.

I made three mistakes.

The grid is lost, but “One more chance” takes you back to your grid with two mistakes, once per grid. You can also turn off the 3-mistake limit or mistake checking in Me → Settings.

Why ads?

They fund the game's development, fixes and new features. The banner stays at the bottom of the screen; videos only appear if you choose them. If no video is available, the help is free.

How do I change my ad choices?

In the game: Me → Settings → Ad choices (in Europe).

Is my progress saved?

Yes, on your phone, after every move. It is erased if you delete the game.
